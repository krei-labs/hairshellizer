Our Story

About HairShellizer

"HairShellizer" derives its name from its two raw organic materials: natural hair and eggshells. By converting these biodegradable wastes into fertilizer rich in essential nutrients, HairShellizer provides a dual benefit: promoting plant vitality and reducing domestic waste. The product is specifically designed for decorative gardeners, providing an effective, natural, and durable solution for healthy plant growth.
