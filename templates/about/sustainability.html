From Local Waste to Useful Growth

Sustainability at HairShellizer

HairShellizer gives biodegradable materials a practical second use by collecting natural hair and eggshells that would otherwise be discarded. Our approach connects waste reduction, local sourcing, responsible production, and accessible gardening in one simple circular process.

Waste Reuse

Biodegradable hair and eggshells are redirected into a useful gardening product instead of being treated only as household waste.

Local Sourcing

Raw materials are collected through local businesses and households in Tanauan City and Sto. Tomas, Batangas.

Garden-Focused

The recovered materials are prepared into a practical fertilizer option for home gardeners and ornamental plants.

Material 01

Human Hair

Hair is collected from local barbershops and salons, where clean natural hair clippings are commonly generated as part of regular services.
