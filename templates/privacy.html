Privacy Policy

HairShellizer collects only the information needed to process your orders and respond to your inquiries: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and order/payment details you provide at checkout.

Your information is used solely to fulfill orders, communicate with you about your orders or inquiries, and improve our service. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, other than the extent needed to operate the site (for example, image storage for product photos).

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored or displayed in plain text. Order and account information is only visible to you and to the HairShellizer seller/admin account.

This is a general policy for a small local business website and is not a substitute for formal legal advice.
